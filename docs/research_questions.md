# Research Question

What genetic variants, genes, and biological pathways
are associated with endometriosis in published genomic studies?

## Objectives

1. Identify peer-reviewed genomic studies on endometriosis.
2. Curate reported genetic associations and study metadata.
3. Compare genetic associations across studies.
4. Visualize the strongest reported associations.
5. Explore biological pathways implicated by the findings.

## Data sources

- GWAS Catalog
- Published supplementary tables
- Publicly available GWAS summary statistics

## Important limitation

This project uses secondary data. Associations identified in
published studies do not by themselves establish causality.
